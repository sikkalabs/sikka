# SIKKA — shut down

**This project is closed. The code has been deleted. There is nothing left here to
build, run, or contribute to.**

Sikka was a 0-fee cryptocurrency for humans, AI agents, and micropayments. It
was named after *sikkā*, the Punjabi word for coin in the history of
[Sikh coinage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikh_coinage), borrowed from
Persian, where it meant both a die for minting and, by extension, the authority
to coin money.

---

## Why

On 14 September we received a letter from the Government of India rejecting our
business MOA. The practical result: we cannot register a company in our own
country.

We want to be plain about how that feels. A team that spent its time building
something gets told, in writing, that it may not exist here as a legal entity.
There was no appeal worth the cost, and no clear answer to the only question
that mattered: *what would have made this acceptable?*

We are a small team and this was never more than a few people trying to build
something. But the frustration is real and it is worth saying out loud: a
process that cannot explain its own requirements has already told you
everything you need to know about building inside it.

## Nobody is harmed

We were in alpha. There is no user data, no customer funds, no outstanding
commitments, and no users depending on this. Nothing breaks. The code and its
history are being removed rather than left to rot in a public repository.

## What's next

We're moving. Not because the idea was wrong, and not because we were told the
technology doesn't work — because a state that won't tell you *why* it says no
is not a place to bet a career on. We'll build where an answer is an actual
answer.

## No ill will

To the officials who reviewed the application: this isn't about you personally.
You processed what you were given, within a system you didn't design. The
failure is the system's, and systems are built by people — which means they can
be unbuilt and rebuilt by people too.

If you're reading this and you're thinking about doing the same thing: it's
knowable in advance that this will be hard. Ask the clarifying question early.
If the answer you get is silence, that silence is the answer.

— **Sikka Labs**
